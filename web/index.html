        Tips

        • Black cuts through, red marks a line, blue engraves (fills). The laser always runs Engrave, then Mark, then Cut through.
        • The green dot is the top-right corner of your design. Move the laser head there on the machine.
        • Always Frame first: the laser traces the outline with the beam off, so you can check it fits your material.
        • Open starts over with a file. Import adds a file next to what's there.
        • Text inside an SVG must be converted to paths first, or use the Text tool.
        • Cutting out letters? Use the Stencil font so O, A and B keep their middles.
        • Type a size, or a Scale % (100 = original size). Switch between mm, cm and inches at the right of the size bar.
        • Change the material from the panel on the right or the buttons at the bottom.
        • Click a colour (bottom bar or the Layers list) to change the selected parts to it.
        • Pattern makes rows and columns of copies, like a sheet of keychains.
        • Your design is saved on this Chromebook, so a reload won't lose it.
        • Stay with the laser the whole time, and know where the red emergency stop is. This site is not a safety system: your teacher checks every job and watches the laser.
