# Using the laser from your Chromebook

Print this and post it next to the laser.

## Draw with three colours
| Colour | What the laser does |
|---|---|
| **Black** | **Cut through**: cuts the part out |
| **Red** | **Mark**: draws a thin line on the surface |
| **Blue** | **Engrave**: fills in the shape |

The laser always does Engrave first, then Mark, then Cut through. Cut through is last because
pieces that are cut free can move.

## Steps
1. Go to **uploadmylaser.com** in Chrome. When it asks, type the class phrase your teacher gives you.
2. On the right, **pick your material**.
3. Add your design:
   - **Open…** starts over with one SVG or DXF file.
   - **Import…** adds another file next to what's already there, like a logo beside your name.
   - **Text**, **Box**, **Circle**, **Line**, **Curve** and **Shapes** on the left. Pick the colour at the bottom first.
   - Stuck? The **ⓘ Help** button at the top lists every shortcut.
4. Drag parts (or the purple grip under a part) to arrange them. Drag the white squares to resize; the
   lock keeps the shape. Type a size in mm or a Scale %. **Pattern** makes rows of copies. **Undo** fixes mistakes.
   Scroll or pinch to zoom.
5. Wait your turn. Plug the laser's USB cable into your Chromebook and click **Connect laser**.
6. Move the laser head to where the **top-right corner** of your design should go (the green dot).
7. Click **Frame (laser off)**. Watch the head trace a box. Make sure it fits on your material.
8. Close the lid. Tick **"I will stay with the laser"**. Click **Send to laser**.
9. **Stay with the laser the whole time.** If anything looks wrong, press **STOP** (or Esc), or the
   red emergency stop button on the laser.

## Getting an SVG from other apps
- **Tinkercad**: select your shapes, **Export → .SVG**. Black lines come out as Cut through.
- **Canva**: **Share → Download → SVG** (needs Canva for Education). Make shapes pure black, red or blue.
- **Inkscape**: **File → Save As → Plain SVG**. Convert text first: **Path → Object to Path**.
- **Google Drawings**: **File → Download → SVG**.

If the app asks "What should these colours do?", your file used a different colour. Pick what it
should be, or **Skip** it.

## If something goes wrong
- **No laser in the list when you click Connect:** unplug the cable, plug it back in, and try again.
  Close any other tab that's using the laser.
- **"The laser is closed":** ask your teacher for today's class phrase.
- **Text in your SVG was skipped:** convert the text to paths first, or use the Text tool.
